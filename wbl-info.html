About Work-Based Learning (WBL)

Bridging academic theory with professional industry practice.

Overview

What is Work-Based Learning?

Work-Based Learning (WBL) is a structured educational approach that integrates academic study with professional practice in real-world settings. It allows students to translate theoretical knowledge acquired in the classroom into workplace applications, thereby enhancing both technical understanding and practical competency.

WBL encompasses various formats, including internships, apprenticeships, cooperative education, or other supervised industry-based training experiences. Its primary objective is to develop workplace readiness by equipping students with relevant experience, strengthening professional attributes, and facilitating a smooth transition from higher education to employment.

Aims of WBL

Develop graduates with high-level, industry-relevant skills by integrating academic learning with authentic workplace experience.

Industry Collaboration

Employers contribute to programme design, learning delivery, student support, and assessment to ensure effective outcomes.

Methodology

Implementation of WBL

The three core components of WBL delivery ensure a comprehensive learning experience.

Industrial Guidance

Provided by the Industry Coach (IC) who serves as a mentor. Students gain hands-on workplace experience by applying theoretical knowledge to practical tasks under the IC's expertise.

Guided Learning

Structured learning activities between lecturers and students as per the official Class Schedule. Includes presentations, discussions, Q&A sessions, and self-directed study.

Independent Learning

Takes place outside formal schedules to cultivate lifelong learning skills. Students proactively source information, deepen understanding, and engage in collaborative problem-solving.

Requirements

Duration & Eligibility

Program Duration

  • Total Duration: 1 Year (46 Weeks)
  • Structure: 2 Regular Semesters (14 weeks each) + 1 Short Semester
  • Calendar: Follows the official UTHM Academic Calendar
  • *Duration may be adjusted by the faculty in certain cases.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Permitted to enrol after successfully completing all university courses (subject to HOD approval).
  • Not allowed to register for any other courses during the WBL period.
  • Must attend the WBL orientation session conducted by the industry department before commencement.
Framework

Roles and Responsibilities

A collaborative effort between the University and Industry partners.

University (UTHM)

WBL Coordinator (Faculty)

  • Drafting the overall WBL plan after discussions with relevant implementers involved in the training program.
  • Ensuring the successful implementation of the WBL according to the faculty’s design.
  • Ensuring that matters related to the implementation of the WBL are conducted in accordance with the relevant quality procedures and requirements.
  • Developing and coordinating feedback forms for students, industry coach, and university supervisors for the purpose of Continuous Quality Improvement (CQI) in WBL.

WBL Coordinator (Program)

  • Assisting in the operational aspects of WBL program’s management.
  • Updating the list of students enrolled in WBL courses and identifying their respective fields.
  • Updating the list of organizations/industries registered by students.
  • Serving as an intermediary between university supervisors, industry coach and students.
  • Providing course code information, estimating the number of students, proposing dates, WBL duration, and training venue details to the department.
  • Creating a schedule for University Supervision.
  • Conducting briefings on WBL operations for university supervisors and students.
  • Compiling activity reports for the overall implementation of WBL.
  • Delivering briefings on instructional materials to instructors and university supervisors.
  • Collecting WBL assessment scores from industry coach.
  • Maintaining records and reports related to WBL instruction.
  • Facilitating course alignment sessions with industry coordinator and university supervisors.
  • Providing a Study Guide and relevant teaching documents.

University Course Instructor

  • Conducting T&L activities based on modules and study guides.
  • Monitoring student progress and evaluating final portfolios.

University Supervisor

  • Establishing meeting schedules (in-person or online).
  • Assessing industrial project proposals and final reports.
  • Coordinating, guiding, and mentoring students.
  • Monitoring and identifying challenges faced by students.
Industry Partner

Industry Coordinator

  • Acting as Liaison Officer between UTHM, students, and coaches.
  • Identifying and selecting suitable Industry Coach(es).
  • Assisting students in planning their placement at the industry.
  • Resolving issues related to WBL students in the workplace.
  • Maintaining regular and effective communication between university supervisors, industry coach(es), and students.
  • Coordinating the consistency of supervisory methods.
  • Providing information to the faculty regarding the distribution list of Industry Coach(es) to students.
  • Serving as an intermediary between the university and organizations/industries.
  • Coordinating student files.
  • Coordinating the implementation of teaching and learning (T&L) at the industry.
  • Coordinating the student evaluation process and submitting the reports to the UTHM within the stipulated time frame.

Industry Coach (IC)

  • Provide training and guidance to students according to the course requirements.
  • Conduct course assessments and monitor students’ progress.
  • Guide students in their preparation for reports/assignments.
  • Ensure that assessment reports are submitted to the Industry Coordinator within the stipulated time frame.
  • Ensure that students fully always comply with the safety and health regulations in the workplace.
  • Review students' log books.
  • Determine appropriate placements and task details for students.
  • Provide instruction and guidance regarding assigned tasks.
  • Evaluate students' performance using university-provided evaluation forms.
  • Submit student evaluations to the university within the specified timeframe.
  • Inform the industry coordinator and faculty of any issues concerning students during their training in the organization/industry.
  • Co-supervise the students' final year project course.
Student (UTHM)

Student

  • Committing to executing every WBL activity during placement.
  • Adhering to confidentiality, safety regulations, and codes of conduct.
  • Completing all designated learning hours and assessments.
