About the Module

WBL Module Overview

The Work-Based Learning (WBL) Module for the Bachelor of Science in Computational Data Analytics with Honours serves as a comprehensive guide to the implementation of WBL, providing clear and structured guidelines for students, academic supervisors, industry coordinators, and industry coaches involved in the one-year industrial training programme.

Developed by the Computational Data Analytics panel from the Department of Mathematics and Statistics, Faculty of Applied Sciences and Technology, this module is designed to strengthen the connection between academic learning and real-world industry practices.

For Students

Guidelines on WBL expectations, responsibilities, and assessment criteria

For Academic Supervisors

Roles, responsibilities, and monitoring procedures for student supervision

For Industry Coordinators

Coordination procedures and industry partnership guidelines

For Industry Coaches

Mentoring guidelines and student assessment frameworks
